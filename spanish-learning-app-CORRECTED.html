Understanding the Difference

⏹️ PRETERITE (Simple Past)
One-time, completed action at a specific point in time
Example: "Ayer hablé con tus padres" (Yesterday I spoke with your parents - it happened once, it's done)
🔄 IMPERFECT (Past Habitual/Background)
Repeated, ongoing, or habitual actions in the past; setting the scene
Example: "Cuando era niño, hablaba español todos los días" (When I was a child, I used to speak Spanish every day - habitual)

Complete Time Marker Guide

🔴 PRETERITE (Point in Time / Completed Action)

Use preterite for:

  • Specific completed action: "Hablé con tus padres" (I spoke with your parents - done)
  • Action that started and ended: "Trabajé 8 horas" (I worked 8 hours - finished)
  • Series of completed events: "Llegué, comí, y me fui" (I arrived, ate, and left)
  • Change of state: "Se enamoró de mí" (She fell in love with me - moment it changed)
Time markers:
  • ayer (yesterday)
  • anoche (last night)
  • la semana pasada (last week)
  • el mes pasado (last month)
  • hace + time (ago): Hace 3 años
  • el año pasado (last year)
  • de repente (suddenly)
  • cuando + specific event (when something happened)

🔵 IMPERFECT (Background / Habitual / Ongoing)

Use imperfect for:

  • Repeated/habitual actions: "Hablaba español todos los días" (I used to speak Spanish every day)
  • Background description: "Era una noche hermosa" (It was a beautiful night - setting the scene)
  • Ongoing action interrupted: "Hablaba cuando él llegó" (I was speaking when he arrived)
  • What someone was doing: "¿Qué hacías?" (What were you doing?)
  • Mental/emotional state in past: "Estaba feliz en esa época" (I was happy during that time)
Time markers:
  • todos los días (every day)
  • siempre (always)
  • frecuentemente (frequently)
  • mientras (while)
  • cada vez que (each time)
  • cuando + state/condition (when I was...)
  • solía + infinitive (used to)
  • en esa época (during that time)

🎯 Quick Memory Trick

Preterite = Photo (snapshot of one moment)
Imperfect = Video (ongoing process, background, habit)

📖 Real Family Examples

Meeting her parents story:

"Hace dos años, fui a su casa por primera vez. (Preterite - specific event) Mientras conducía, estaba muy nervioso. (Imperfect - state I was in) Cuando llegué, su madre preparaba la cena. (Imperfect - what she was doing) Hablamos durante tres horas. (Preterite - completed action) Fue una experiencia increíble. (Preterite - one-time event)"
